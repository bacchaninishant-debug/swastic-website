The Swastic Exports Promise

A promise that what you hold is not just rare and beautiful — but rarest, clearest, brightest version of what nature intended.

✦
01

100% Natural. Ethical. Trusted.

Every single SWASTIC EXPORTS natural gemstone is ethically sourced in accordance with the Kimberley Process certification scheme and United Nations resolutions. We partner solely with artisanal mining co-operatives and direct-from-mine origins in Zambia, Sri Lanka, and Mozambique that prioritize social responsibility, fair compensation, and environmental rehabilitation.

02

100% Pure

Pure crystalline gemstone treasures formed by earth over millions of years. We never sell treated, synthetic-filled, resin-infused, or fracture-filled stones. What you receive from Swastic Exports is pure, unadulterated brilliance with authentic mine-to-market provenance.

03

Integrity of Clarity

Every precious gemstone and diamond is independently authenticated and graded by premier international gemological institutions including the Gemological Institute of America (GIA), International Gemological Institute (IGI), and GRS Swisslab. Each stone features verifiable documentation corresponding to its official certificate of authenticity.

04

Crafted Beyond Conviction

True luxury is not manufactured; it is sculpted. Backed by over 30 years of master lapidary cutting, faceting, and bespoke goldsmithing since 1994, our master artisans dedicate between 30 to 60 meticulous hours hand-cutting each facet and hand-forging each solid Platinum 950 or 18K gold setting with bespoke micro-claw pavé.
