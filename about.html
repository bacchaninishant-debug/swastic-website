✦ OUR 30-YEAR HERITAGE ✦

Mastering the Art of Fine Gemstones & Carvings

For over 30 years, Swastic Exports has been bringing the world’s finest gemstones to global jewelry houses and private connoisseurs. From certified Zambian emeralds, fiery rubies, sapphires, to rare semi-precious stones, every single piece is carefully sourced, calibrated, and cut with exacting precision.

Our expertise goes far beyond faceted gems. We also specialize in master handcrafted carvings, gemstone beads, royal drops, and cabochons, sculpted to inspire timeless jewelry creations. With an unyielding focus on sustainability, authenticity, and consistency, designers, collectors, and luxury brands worldwide trust us to deliver brilliance in every form.

“More than gemstones, we deliver heritage, artistry, and enduring value in every stone.”

✦ RARE ZAMBIAN EMERALDS ✦

We Don't Just Sell Gemstones — We Craft Timeless Legacies

At Swastic Exports, we believe that rare gemstones are more than adornments — they are timeless legacies passed through generations. With 30+ years of global trust, we specialize in high-grade certified Zambian emeralds celebrated for their rich forest-green saturation, crystalline transparency, and regal brilliance.

Alongside emeralds, our global atelier offers a wide range of precious & semi-precious treasures including pigeon blood rubies, royal blue sapphires, tourmalines, and fine spinels. Every stone is ethically mined, hand-selected at origin, and cut to geometric perfection by master lapidaries.
